Honest part

What didn’t work — and what I took from it.

Not everything I build ships or wins. Rejected redesigns, stopped projects, pitches that went nowhere — they’re part of the record, so they’re on this page too.

  • 01→

    KINETIK redesign

    Built in a day, but kinetik.kg still runs the old store — switching is the owner’s call.

    A test link in the brand’s Instagram bio got the account logged out. Link brands only from their own domain.

  • 02→

    KOKORO Order

    An Apple-style order site, finished and tested — rejected the next day.

    Keep what already works; confirm the direction with one screen.

  • 03→

    KOKORO in 3D

    A full 3D version of the first site — “the first was better”.

    Show one mockup frame before a big style change.

  • 04→

    HAZE

    A storefront for heated-tobacco devices; the real assortment was vapes, banned in Kyrgyzstan.

    Check the legality of the actual product first.

  • 05

    WAKA

    A vape storefront request, plus a request to hide who built it. Stopped on day one at the spec stage.

    Check that the product is legal before any design; a request to hide authorship is a red flag.

  • 06→

    MK Group

    Three variants of a developer landing in one day; it never launched.

  • 07→

    BREAKING STORE deck

    An animated sales deck for the shop — never sent, price slot still empty.

  • 08→

    BULUT

    A 27-slide investor deck for my studio. No investor meetings and no paying clients yet.

  • 09→

    Live Club Map

    Researched and pitched, but no club signed.

    Build only after one or two prepayments.

  • 10→

    Arisong

    A music player whose scope changed four times in two days; never published.
