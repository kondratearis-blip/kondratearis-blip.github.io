About

Hi, I’m Aris.

AI Application Developer & Business Automation Specialist, based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. I build web apps, automation, AI tools and video — for a delivery business, for the drink brand I work at, and for my own products.

01

I learn by shipping

Since October 2026 I’ve built 36 projects — production systems, own products, brand sites, decks and video. Every one is on this site, with an honest status.

AI-assisted development with Claude Code is my daily process — together with specs before code, automated tests, independent reviews and releases with rollback.

02

Real businesses

KOKORO, a food-delivery business in Bishkek, runs on the ordering system I built: customer app, manager panel, kitchen screen and courier app. I release improvements almost every day together with the owner.

At KINETIK, a Kyrgyz sports-drink brand, I’m in the team that makes the website and Reels.

03

Honesty is a rule

Every number on this site comes from project files. I don’t publish clients’ revenue and I don’t claim results I can’t show — rejected and stopped projects are listed too.

I check legality and rights before building: I turned down vape storefronts, use only licensed music and keep other people’s logos off my work.

04

Why Japan

I want to grow inside an international engineering team, learn from senior developers and work on products used by many people. Japan’s technology industry — its attention to quality and long-term thinking — is where I want to build that experience.

I’m open to a junior or internship role in Japan. Until a work visa is possible, I’m happy to start remotely or as a contractor.
