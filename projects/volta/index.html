VOLTA — electronics store UI

An electronics storefront that borrows the UX of a banking app: stories, promo banners, installment pricing and a cart drawer.

The story

A UI study: what if buying a laptop felt as quick as paying in a bank app?

My role. Design and build.

Client
UI/UX demo
When
Oct 2026
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript@imgly/background-removal-node

What I built

  1. 01Stories with autoplay and hold-to-pause, swipeable promos, category tiles.
  2. 02Catalog with filters, search, sorting and favourites; product page with colours and “pay now / in parts”.
  3. 03Cart drawer with an installment calculator; light and dark themes.
  4. 0425 Wikimedia Commons photos with backgrounds removed (1.2 MB in total) and a credits page.

Outcome

A UI demo, not a real shop: prices are approximate and three models still use SVG drawings.
