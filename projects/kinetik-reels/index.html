KINETIK — Reels

Re-editing an ad that got no reach, and a 37-second explainer with motion graphics synced to speech.

The story

Short-form video for the brand: diagnosing why a Reel didn’t work, rebuilding it hook-first, and an explainer “What is an isotonic?” with graphics that land exactly on the spoken words.

My role. Editing, motion graphics, sound, subtitles and the render pipeline.

What I built

  1. 01Diagnosis of the original: an 8.7-second static opening, no on-screen text, the product only at 19 s, no call to action.
  2. 02Hook-first re-edit: beat-synced cuts and punch-in zooms every 1.5–2 s, in three cuts — main, a short one for A/B testing and one without music.
  3. 03Kyrgyz speech recognised with a Kyrgyz Whisper model and turned into Russian word-by-word subtitles.
  4. 04Explainer with six animated cards over licensed stock and electrolyte tiles that land on the spoken words.
  5. 05Graphics written in HTML/JS as a function of time, rendered by headless Chrome to transparent frames and composited with ffmpeg.
  6. 06Voice separated from gym noise with Demucs, music ducking, loudness −14 LUFS; licensed Mixkit music only — requests to use commercial hits were declined.
  7. 07A third interview Reel is finished but not shown here: it waits for the interviewee’s consent.

Outcome

Delivered as files. Publication and reach were not recorded, so I don’t claim performance numbers.
