KOKORO Order — Apple-style concept

A scroll scene where the bucket lights up, and an Apple Store-like configurator that sends a ready WhatsApp order.

The story

An attempt to make the order page feel premium with the brand’s own footage — no AI-generated food, as the client asked.

My role. Design, media pipeline, build and tests.

Client
KOKORO
When

  2. 02Order configurator → a prefilled WhatsApp message with an order number.
  3. 03Automated order test: 59 of 59 checks at 375 and 1440 px.

Outcome

What I took from it
