AI Business Assistant

Reads customer messages, classifies them, drafts a reply and a task — a person approves before anything happens.

The story

Small businesses get dozens of messages a day across WhatsApp, email and the website. This prototype shows the pattern I use for AI in operations: AI prepares, a human decides.

My role. Concept, interface and implementation. The AI mode uses the Claude API with structured output; without a key the demo uses a transparent rule engine.

What I built

  1. 01Request classification: order, complaint, question, booking, feedback or spam — with urgency, sentiment and language.
  2. 02Suggested reply in the customer’s language that avoids promises the business can’t keep.
  3. 03Task with owner and deadline, created only after approval.
  4. 04Conversation history with every message, classification and approved reply.
